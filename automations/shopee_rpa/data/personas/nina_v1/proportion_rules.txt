IDENTITY AND BODY PROPORTION RULES:
Preserve the same woman from the saved base references: face identity, hair, skin tone, age appearance and overall silhouette. persona_master.png is face authority only: do not copy its close-selfie camera geometry onto the body. nina_360_master.png is the body and physical-detail authority; use its standing views for body length and silhouette. Scene/style images have no identity authority.
Keep natural human proportions and a realistic head-to-body ratio. Do not enlarge the head or face. Keep shoulders, waist, hips, arms and legs proportionate and coherent with the base reference. Maintain a slim-to-average realistic feminine build, following the user's modestly slimmer direction without progressively shrinking her between generations. Avoid a heavier interpretation unless explicitly requested. No oversized upper body, exaggerated chest, widened waist, short limbs or inflated anatomy. Preserve the tattoo, natural curves and body mass distribution.
CAMERA / LENS / DISTANCE RULES:
Use realistic smartphone-photo logic and a standard main-camera look, never an exaggerated ultra-wide or fisheye. Camera distance controls perspective; merely saying 35mm does not repair a close-range composition. Avoid very close framing that enlarges the face, foreground arm or upper body. Keep the subject near the optical center and the camera approximately level, at eye level or slightly below/above as appropriate. Do not lean the upper body toward the lens. Scene-specific distances below are production defaults to test, not measured guarantees.
For standing mirror selfies: subject about 1.5–2 m from a flat vertical mirror; phone near upper chest with relaxed natural arm bend; camera nearly parallel to the mirror, no dramatic tilt. Include the whole head, feet and floor margin; keep natural body length and leg proportions. Use sufficient room to fit the body instead of switching to ultra-wide.
For direct handheld selfies: arm comfortably extended about 0.7–0.9 m, lens near eye height, face centered, head/shoulders/torso balanced; no foreground arm filling the image. If the intended crop cannot fit naturally, use a supported phone/timer farther away instead of inventing arm length or wide-angle perspective.
For supported-phone routine shots: use about 1.8–2.5 m working distance and balanced half/three-quarter/full-body framing. A closer crop must come from a farther capture, never from moving the lens against the face.
COMPOSITION RULES:
Keep full or half body visually balanced. Do not crop to make the head oversized relative to the torso. Keep torso and legs coherent, preserve standing body length, avoid edge stretching and unnatural mirror reflections. These constraints override conflicting close, high-angle or diagonal scene cues; preserve the scene by moving the camera or widening the framing.
REALISM RULES:
Realistic skin texture, natural light, coherent shadows, natural posture, realistic hands, realistic body mass distribution. No AI-like swelling, plastic skin or inflated anatomy. A beautiful face is not sufficient to approve anatomy.
