# Cenas e prompts adaptáveis

Os exemplos são receitas visuais. Ajuste pessoa, roupa, enquadramento e ambiente ao pedido. Em uma série, substitua a descrição de pessoa pela identidade estabelecida e anexe a referência disponível.

## Quarto com luz de janela — teste aprovado

O usuário aprovou a imagem produzida com esta combinação de decisões. Esta versão condensada preserva os elementos centrais do prompt usado, sem implicar que outras variações já tenham sido testadas.

```text
Create one vertical casual smartphone mirror selfie of an adult woman around 28, enjoying a relaxed moment at home. Ordinary bedroom with a slightly rumpled bed and a casually draped item of clothing. Loosely tied brown hair with a few flyaways, comfortable oversized cotton T-shirt and soft lounge shorts. Relaxed shoulders, weight resting on one leg, a spontaneous half-smile while looking at the phone screen. One hand holds a plain smartphone partly overlapping one cheek in the reflection; the other arm hangs comfortably.

Portrait 3:4 framing from head to just below the knees, slightly imperfect handheld composition, moderate wide-angle smartphone perspective around 26 mm equivalent, recognizable bedroom background. Available side-window daylight, modest uneven exposure, slightly dimmer background and realistic shadow falloff. Subtle pores appropriate to the viewing distance, faint skin imperfections, natural tonal variation and skin highlights, believable cotton folds, mild sensor grain in shadows and subtle phone compression.

Keep head-to-body proportions believable, hands anatomically coherent, grip plausible and mirror reflection physically consistent. Avoid beauty retouching, waxy skin, excessive pore sharpening, studio lighting, strong portrait-mode bokeh, exaggerated proportions, extra digits, duplicated phones or people, text and watermarks.
```

## Penteadeira com luz superior

```text
Casual smartphone mirror selfie of [adult subject or supplied character reference], framed from the waist up at a home vanity. Wearing [casual clothing], looking at the phone screen with [simple relaxed expression]. The phone is held naturally with a plausible grip. Overhead vanity bulbs create small highlights on the forehead and nose, with a dimmer doorway in the background. A few cosmetics and a hairbrush sit on the counter. Natural skin tone variation, subtle pores, realistic clothing folds, recognizable surroundings and slight grain in darker areas. Coherent mirror geometry, natural head-to-body proportions and anatomically plausible hands. Avoid heavy retouching, waxy skin and studio portrait styling.
```

## Quarto com pouca luz ou flash

Escolha uma das duas situações de luz e adapte as restrições à escolha. Um clarão localizado é esperado quando o flash aparece no espelho.

```text
Relaxed full-body smartphone mirror selfie of [adult subject or supplied character reference] in an everyday bedroom, wearing [casual outfit]. Weight shifted comfortably to one leg, phone partly covering the face, gaze toward the screen. Entire figure visible with plausible proportions and a coherent reflection. A slightly unmade bed and a few personal belongings remain recognizable in the background.

[Lighting option A: Low warm bedroom ambient light, believable dim exposure and subtle sensor grain in shadows.]
[Lighting option B: Direct phone flash reflected in the mirror, a localized bright glare and crisp shadows consistent with the flash position.]

Natural skin detail at this distance, relaxed posture and believable fabric texture. Avoid excessive skin smoothing, artificial body reshaping, duplicated limbs and implausible phone reflections.
```

## Banheiro com iluminação fria

```text
Everyday smartphone mirror selfie of [adult subject or supplied character reference], framed from the head to the hips in a home bathroom. Wearing [requested clothing], holding the phone naturally and looking at its screen. Cool overhead bathroom lighting with a modest contribution from a mirror light, believable shadows around the collarbones and under the chin. Neutral tiles, a counter and a chrome faucet establish the setting. Natural skin texture and tonal variation, fine image grain, broad depth of field and slightly imperfect handheld framing. Preserve coherent reflection geometry, believable body proportions and hand anatomy. Avoid heavy airbrushing, exaggerated skin detail and strong studio-style background blur.
```
