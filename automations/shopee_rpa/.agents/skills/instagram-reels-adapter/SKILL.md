# Instagram Reels Adapter Skill

## Purpose
Tailors the creative output and copy for Instagram Reels distribution, directing viewers to the affiliate link in the profile bio or direct messages (DM).

## Adaptation Rules
1. **Bio / DM CTA**:
   - Instagram does not allow clickable links inside post descriptions.
   - All CTAs must instruct: "Comente 'QUERO' que te envio o link direto no direct" or "Link disponível no destaque 'Achadinhos' e na bio!".
2. **Copy Pacing**:
   - Clean spacing with line breaks for readability.
   - Short summary of why the item is useful without jargon.
3. **Hashtag Selection**:
   - Instagram algorithmic tags: #reelsbrasil #dicasdecasa #achadinhos #shopeebr + category tags.
   - Limit to 5-10 relevant tags.
