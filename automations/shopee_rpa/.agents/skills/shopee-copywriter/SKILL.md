# Shopee Copywriter Skill

## Purpose
Generates localized Portuguese (pt-BR) copy specifically formatted for Shopee Video commerce cards, post captions, and comments.

## Copywriting Framework
1. **Shopee Video Caption**:
   - Punchy opening statement highlighting key practical benefit.
   - 2-3 bullet points with confirmed features (e.g. material, dimensions, voltage).
   - Clear CTA to tap the product basket on the screen ("Toque na sacolinha laranja para ver valores e frete!").
2. **Pinned Comment**:
   - Direct reinforcement of where to find the product card and coupon information.
3. **Hashtag Strategy**:
   - #AchadinhosShopee #ShopeeBrasil #ComprasShopee #Achadinhos + 2-3 niche tags matching the product category.
   - Max 8 hashtags to avoid spam penalty.
