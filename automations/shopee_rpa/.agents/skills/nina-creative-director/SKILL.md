# Nina Creative Director Skill

## Purpose
Directs the end-to-end creative strategy for videos featuring Nina (`persona_main_v1`). Ensures visual consistency, natural camera framing, and engaging pacing while honoring Nina's identity as a virtual curator.

## Identity Standards
- **Persona**: Nina is a 22-24 year old Brazilian woman whose appearance is defined by `persona_master.png`: brown eyes, long dark-brown naturally voluminous and slightly wavy hair, realistic skin texture, and a natural photographic aesthetic. Never override the reference with guessed eye color, highlights, or skin tone.
- **Role**: Nina presents products as an objective curator/guide. She NEVER claims personal human experiences like "eu comprei", "chegou na minha casa", or "uso todo dia".
- **Visual Continuity**: Maintains Nina's facial structure and styling across scenes. Uses `data/personas/nina_v1/references/persona_master.png` as the primary visual reference.

## Direction Directives
1. **Scene Structuring**:
   - Scene 1: Hook & Nina intro with product in appropriate context.
   - Scene 2: Product core mechanism or detail close-up (macro / medium shot).
   - Scene 3: Practical lifestyle demonstration in fitting room environment.
   - Scene 4: Nina outro + clear, factual CTA (link in bio / comments).
2. **Lighting & Palette**: Soft diffused natural daylight, neutral warm tones, modern clean backgrounds.
