# Photorealism Rule

1. **Camera & Optics**:
   - Prompts must specify raw camera parameters: "35mm photograph, shot on Sony A7 IV, 50mm f/1.8 lens, natural depth of field".
   - Visible depth blur (bokeh), soft realistic focus, natural sensor noise/film grain.
2. **Lighting**:
   - Diffused daylight from window, soft bounce, realistic shadow fall-off.
   - Avoid dramatic studio neon lights unless specifically called for by product aesthetic.
3. **Compulsory Negative Prompts**:
   - `CGI, 3D render, cartoon, anime, illustration, doll-like, plastic skin, airbrushed, wax figure, extra fingers, missing fingers, deformed hands, warped text, watermarks, oversaturated colors`.
4. **Anatomy Control**:
   - Scrutinize hands, fingers, and eyes in every prompt and QA pass. Ensure realistic human scale and grip on objects.
