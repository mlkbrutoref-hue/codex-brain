# Content Style Rule

1. **Language & Dialect**:
   - Spoken Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR), natural and friendly, suitable for modern social media.
   - Avoid overly formal academic Portuguese, translation calques, or regional slangs that limit reach.
2. **Pacing & Cadence**:
   - Sentences must be short, punchy, and easily spoken aloud within 3-4 seconds per scene.
   - Total narration should range between 40 and 65 words for a 15-20 second reel.
3. **Engagement Architecture**:
   - 0-3s: Immediate visual & auditory hook addressing a real curiosity or common daily friction.
   - 3-8s: Clear presentation of the solution/mechanism.
   - 8-15s: Contextual demonstration in a relatable setting.
   - 15-20s: Natural, non-aggressive call to action (check link in bio / comment).
4. **Anti-Hype**:
   - No screaming caps, no fake countdowns, no manufactured crises. Maintain trust and authenticity.
