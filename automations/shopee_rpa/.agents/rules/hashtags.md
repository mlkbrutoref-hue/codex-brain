# Hashtags Rule

1. **Tag Density**:
   - Limit to 5-8 carefully chosen hashtags per post. Avoid hashtag stuffing (>10 tags), which triggers algorithmic spam suppression.
2. **Composition Formula**:
   - 2 High-Volume Core Tags: `#achadinhos`, `#shopeebrasil`
   - 2 Medium-Volume Discovery Tags: `#achadinhosshopee`, `#dicasdecasa` (or `#comprinhas`)
   - 2-3 Niche Specific Tags: matched strictly to product domain (e.g. `#organizacao`, `#papelaria`, `#tecnologia`, `#beleza`)
3. **Blacklist**:
   - Never use banned, irrelevant, or generic clickbait tags: `#foryou`, `#fy`, `#viral`, `#followme`, `#like4like`.
